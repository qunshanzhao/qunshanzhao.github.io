I am deeply honoured to receive the **[2026 CPGIS Young Scholar Award](https://www.cpgis.org/Awards)** from the International Association of Chinese Professionals in Geographic Information Sciences (CPGIS), presented at the CPGIS 2026 annual conference in Singapore. The award recognises young "rising stars" for their research contributions to GIScience who received their doctoral degree in the last 10 years.

From the award citation:

<blockquote>
  <p style="margin-bottom:1em;">Professor Qunshan Zhao is Professor of Urban Analytics and Deputy Director of the Urban Big Data Centre at the University of Glasgow, United Kingdom. Since receiving his PhD in Geography and GIScience from Arizona State University in 2017, he has established himself as a leading young scholar in GIScience in the United Kingdom and Europe.</p>
  <p style="margin-bottom:1em;">His interdisciplinary research combines urban analytics and artificial intelligence, spatial optimization, and urban sensing to address critical urban challenges, including climate adaptation, building energy efficiency, fuel poverty, healthy housing, electric vehicle infrastructure, and informal-settlement mapping. He has published 58 refereed journal articles and secured nearly £10 million in research funding as Principal Investigator and Co-Investigator from major organizations, including the UK Economic and Social Research Council, the Bill &amp; Melinda Gates Foundation, and the European Space Agency.</p>
  <p style="margin-bottom:1em;">Professor Zhao's work has informed heat and clean-air policies in London and Glasgow. He has also made important contributions through student mentorship, open-source software development, and professional leadership. His exceptional achievements have been recognized by the University of Glasgow, where he progressed from Lecturer to full Professor within five years.</p>
  <p style="margin-bottom:0;"><strong>In recognition of his outstanding contributions to GIScience through research, education, mentorship, and professional service, it is our great pleasure to present the 2026 CPGIS Young Scholar Award to Professor Qunshan Zhao. Please join me in congratulating him on this well-deserved honor.</strong></p>
</blockquote>

Thank you to CPGIS, and to all my mentors, collaborators, and students — this recognition belongs to the whole lab. The award was featured in [University of Glasgow news](https://www.gla.ac.uk/news/headline_1286240_en.html), and more photos are in my [LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7485146693399674881/).
